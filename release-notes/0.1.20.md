# Superiority 0.1.20

Live: your open channels, on a web page you can hand to anyone.

## Live

- Turn on Live in Settings and Superiority streams your open channels to a
  page of your own at live.superioritybot.com. Messages, joins and leaves,
  and the member list appear there seconds after they happen.
- The page is the app, in a browser: the same tabs, transcript, roster,
  portraits, and presence, drawn with the same art.
- Your link is minted the first time you turn Live on and shown beside the
  switch. Anyone with the link can watch; nobody can guess it.
- Live is off until you turn it on. Turning it off stops the stream, and
  closing a tab takes that channel off the page.
- Whispers, friends, and blocked accounts are never sent.
- Join and leave notices follow your own setting: hidden in your transcript
  means hidden on the page. The member list stays current either way.

## Friends

- A friend's standing updates when it changes, rather than when something else
  happens to refresh the list.
