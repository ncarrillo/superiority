# Superiority 0.1.26

This release focuses on dependable party support and clearer channel identity
across Superiority and Superiority Live.

## Parties

- Party invitations now identify who sent them.
- Accepting an invitation now joins the party reliably without disconnecting.
- Party chat opens in its own channel instead of replacing an existing channel.
- Invitations continue to work when the normal channel limit is full.
- Improved party membership, presence, and chat behavior after joining.
- Refined the invitation layout for clearer information and actions.

## Channels and Superiority Live

- Party channels are consistently named **Party**.
- Party channels now use a distinct pink tab treatment.
- Group channels now use a distinct orange tab treatment.
- Group names remain resolved correctly in Superiority Live, including after
  reconnecting.
- Prevented party channels from being restored or handled as ordinary chat
  channels.

## Reliability

- Fixed disconnects associated with party invitations and membership changes.
- Improved connection stability while joining and leaving parties.
- Improved Protocol Viewer coverage for party and channel activity.
