# Superiority 0.1.24

This release makes Superiority Live more dependable during reconnects and
temporary network interruptions.

## Live

- Kept the last confirmed channel and member lists visible while reconnecting.
- Improved channel and member accuracy after a connection is restored.
- Made online and offline status updates more timely and reliable.
- Preserved Live chat history across reconnects.
- Fixed stale channels and members lingering after missed joins or leaves.

## Reliability

- Improved recovery from temporary connection failures.
- Improved consistency between the desktop app and Superiority Live.
