# Superiority 0.1.19

Tabs can be dragged into the order you want, and a channel that will not open
says why instead of leaving you guessing.

## Tabs

- Drag a tab along the bar to reorder it. The order is kept, so the channels
  reopen the way you arranged them.
- Closing a tab draws it off the bar rather than making it disappear, and the
  rest close the gap.
- Tab names sit in the middle of the tab.

## When a channel will not open

- The reason arrives as a card rather than a line in whichever transcript
  happened to be in front. It names the group, and says what went wrong: you
  need an invitation, the channel is full, you are already in it, or you are in
  as many channels as Battle.net allows.

## Groups

- A group you have just been invited to appears in Join a channel straight
  away, rather than after the next launch.

## Bug fixes

- A private group is no longer offered as one you can simply join. Privacy was
  being read from the wrong place, so every private group looked open — the
  join was always going to be refused.
- A group's type — Barcraft, Esports Teams, Schools, and the rest — is read
  correctly. It was reporting whether the group was private instead.
- Six channels at once, which is what Battle.net grants. Superiority allowed a
  seventh and let the service refuse it.
