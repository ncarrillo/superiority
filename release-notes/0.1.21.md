# Superiority 0.1.21

The live page keeps up with each channel as it changes, and being removed from
one finally tells you.

## Live

- Portraits, presence, and channel and group names now show on the page and
  keep up as they change.
- A channel you leave, or are removed from, drops off the page.
- A quiet channel stays live while you are connected, rather than reading
  offline until the next message.

## Channels

- Being removed or banned from a channel is no longer silent. A card tells you
  which channel; the tab stays until you close the card, then closes with it.

## Bug fixes

- Names shown on the live page no longer carry the trailing character code.
