# Superiority 0.1.23

This release brings a broad round of interface polish, performance
improvements, and reliability fixes to Superiority and Superiority Live.

## Interface

- Improved control sizing, spacing, alignment, hover states, and pressed
  states throughout the app.
- Made tab switching, tab closing, dialogs, and other transitions smoother and
  more responsive.
- Improved tab reordering and the display of long channel names.
- Prevented open dialogs from passing mouse or keyboard input to the screen
  beneath them.

## Channels and chat

- Improved member-list scrolling, filtering, animated updates, portraits,
  selection, and tooltips.
- Improved transcript scrolling, message wrapping, and text selection.
- Fixed group channels sometimes continuing to show a temporary name after
  joining.
- Fixed group invitations not appearing correctly.
- Selecting an entry in Join a Channel now keeps the dialog open until Join or
  Cancel is selected.

## Social

- Fixed friends and conversations sometimes failing to load.
- Fixed selecting a friend not opening the conversation view.
- Selecting the Social button while the pane is open now closes it.
- Improved conversation transitions and spacing within the Social pane.

## Live

- Improved visual and behavioral consistency between Superiority and
  Superiority Live.
- Improved layouts and touch scrolling on smaller screens.
- Fixed channel switching briefly showing an empty or outdated view.
- Fixed member lists retaining users from an older session.
- The Live link in Settings now opens in the default browser.

## Settings and updates

- Fixed appearance, chat, privacy, and Live settings not surviving an app
  restart.
- Fixed background artwork and tint scaling incorrectly inside chat.
- Improved the update progress and results dialog, including formatted release
  notes and clickable links.

## Bug fixes

- Fixed text fields sometimes failing to accept input.
- Fixed caret placement and text-selection highlighting.
- Improved member-list and transcript scrolling performance.
