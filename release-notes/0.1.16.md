# Superiority 0.1.16

Your friends list is alive, and you can talk to people privately.

## Friends list presence

- The Social pane now shows your Battle.net friends with live status: available,
  away, busy, in game, or offline.
- Friends are grouped into online and offline, each sorted by name, with a
  running count of who is around.
- Every friend carries their portrait, so the list reads at a glance.
- The list stays current while it is open — friends move between the online and
  offline groups as they come and go, with no need to reopen the pane.

## Whispering

- Send a private message with `/w <player> <message>`. The command palette
  offers the command as soon as you type a slash.
- Click any friend in the Social pane to open the composer with the whisper
  already addressed and the cursor in place.
- Private messages are set apart from channel chat in your transcript, so a
  whisper never reads as something you said to the room.
- When a whisper cannot be delivered, Superiority tells you rather than dropping
  it silently.

## Connection and window

- Superiority no longer shows an empty chat window while it is away from
  Battle.net. The tabs, chat, roster, and composer fade in once the connection
  dialog has finished leaving the screen.
- Cancelling a connection, a failed connection, and signing out now keep the
  dialog on screen with a Reconnect action, instead of leaving a window with
  nothing to act on.
- The Social pane opens and closes with the same scan-and-glow transition as
  every other dialog.
