# Superiority 0.1.32

This release makes chat and member-list interactions feel faster and more
direct.

## Chat

- Web addresses in channel transcripts are now clickable and open in the
  system browser.
- Moving the pointer over the composer lets you begin typing without clicking
  first.
- Text fields now display a blinking caret that resets while typing or
  navigating.

## Member list

- Moving the pointer over the member list lets you type immediately to filter
  members.
- Member changes now update in place without making the entire list flash.
- Improved member-list stability during frequent presence and roster updates.

## Appearance

- Channel backgrounds now fill the complete tab content area without extending
  into the tab bar.
- Removed the border and separate panel treatment from the transcript.
- The member list retains its existing panel and border treatment.
