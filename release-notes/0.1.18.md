# Superiority 0.1.18

Pinned home channels are replaced by the channels you have open, public
communities can be found by name, and the disconnects are fixed.

## Channels

- Pinned and home channels are gone. The channels you have open are the channels
  you are in, and they open again next time you launch.
- Seven channels at once, which is as many as Battle.net will address. Joining an
  eighth says so rather than failing quietly.
- Closing a tab leaves the channel and drops it from what reopens.
- A channel that no longer exists is dropped instead of being reopened on every
  launch, and says so rather than hanging on Joining.
- A group tab restores under the group's name rather than its number.

## Finding a channel

- Join a channel searches Battle.net's public communities as you type, alongside
  your own groups and its channel list.
- Your own groups are the suggestions when the box is empty, and are left out of
  the results when it is not — you are already in them.
- A community says whether it can be joined or needs an invitation, and carries
  the icon it was given.
- A community whose name matches a public channel no longer hides the channel;
  both are offered.

## Reading

- A game invitation posted in chat shows the lobby's name instead of the markup
  it arrives as.
- Joining a group says the group's name rather than its number.

## Bug fixes

- Superiority no longer drops the connection while you use it.
- Group and community names are no longer cut short.
- Searching for a channel no longer hangs the app.
- Picking something in the Join a channel list no longer scrolls it back to the
  top.
