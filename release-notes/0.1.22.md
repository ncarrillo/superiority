# Superiority 0.1.22

## Channels

- Fixed a rate-limiting bug that could drop the connection.
- Roster portraits show a placeholder while they load, and fill in as they
  arrive.

## Party invitations

- Initial support: party invitations now show a card you can accept or decline.

## Bug fixes

- Reconnecting no longer leaves the previous conversation on screen until the
  window is resized.
- A reply split across packets could drop the connection under heavy traffic; it
  now waits for the rest.
