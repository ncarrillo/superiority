# Superiority 0.1.17

Whispers move into the Social pane. Clan and community channels can be joined,
and invitations to them answered, from the app.

## Whispers in the Social pane

- Clicking someone in the Social pane opens a conversation with them, instead of
  addressing a whisper in the chat box.
- The conversation shows who you are talking to and their status, with your
  messages and theirs on opposite sides.
- Whispers no longer appear in the channel transcript. They belong to the
  conversation, not to the room you happen to be standing in.
- Anyone you have whispered is collected in a Whispers group above your friends,
  showing the last thing said and a count of what you have not read.
- Images and GIFs linked in a whisper play in the conversation.

## Joining channels

- A Join a channel dialog lists your clan and community channels alongside
  Battle.net's own, and you can search it or type a name outright.

## Clan and community invitations

- An invitation arrives as a card naming the group that invited you, with accept
  and decline on it.

## Everywhere else

- The connection dialog says what it is waiting for in plain terms, and shows
  one progress bar instead of a checklist of internal phases.
- Dialogs open with the same strike and flicker they close with, and the
  disconnect warning burns orange rather than blue.
- The friends list, the channel roster, and the Social pane share one type
  scale, and text sits where it belongs in every input.
- Slash commands are gone: the Join dialog and the Social pane replace `/join`,
  `/leave`, and `/w`, and `/version`, `/clear`, and `/help` are retired.
